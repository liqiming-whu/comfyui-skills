A solitary weathered black-stone gateway rises from a calm grey sea, its eroded lintel and repeated masonry courses large enough to dwarf a tiny wooden rowboat approaching from the lower right. The gateway sits left of center beneath an expansive pearl-grey sky, with empty water and a softly broken reflection giving it room to breathe. A low, unhurried view from a boat with a 50mm lens, slight natural asymmetry in the framing. Soft morning light enters from the left, the near stone retains tactile salt-worn texture, distant water fades into haze. Quiet surreal photography with restrained slate and ivory tones.
