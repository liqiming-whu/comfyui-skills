Two terracotta cities face one another across a narrow horizontal band of open sky: an inverted city hangs from the top edge with its towers pointing down, while its upright counterpart rises from the ground below. Their tallest central spires align tip to tip on the vertical centerline and almost touch, leaving a slim, clearly visible gap; the surrounding roofs step back to open a wider breathing space on either side. A single tiny warm-colored hot-air balloon floats just right of the centerline, halfway between the tips, its envelope and basket readable but dwarfed by both cities. A thin translucent cloud band lies behind the gap without hiding the balloon or the tips. A few waterfalls and fragments rise upward from the inverted streets toward the upper edge, away from the gap. Repeated stone arcades and stepped terraces establish scale, with restrained weathered surfaces, clearer near silhouettes, hazy middle districts and distant towers fading into dusk blue. Low warm dusk sunlight enters from the left, lighting left-facing masonry and casting long shadows to the right across both cities. A distant 24mm wide-angle view, balanced upper and lower city masses, the gap centered vertically.
